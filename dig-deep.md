## Overview

Dig Deep is a finite-resource mode set across 2,968 blocks of depth. You'll start with almost everything locked, so your goal is to reach the bottom while collecting blueprints along the way — find them all to unlock everything the Block Shop and Item Shop have to offer. Your stamina bar drains fast, so save it for quick escapes when things get dangerous. Watch out for mobs that spawn at night, and be ready to upgrade your tools as you descend — rock layers get tougher to mine the deeper you go. Along the way, you can unlock exclusive avatars found only in this mode, like Carpenter, Knight, Cyborg, Sage, and more.

## Getting Started

Find a safe starting area — look for flat, open ground away from caves or ledges before you start digging. This gives you room to build a base and avoid surprise mob encounters early on.
Gather basic resources (wood, stone) — chop nearby trees for wood first, then mine surface stone. You'll need both before crafting your first tools.
Craft tools (pickaxe, shovel, hatchet) — these are essential for gathering resources efficiently. Without them, digging and chopping take much longer.
Light your surroundings with torches — place torches around your starting area and entrance points immediately. Unlit areas are where mobs spawn, even near the surface.
Check your stamina bar — get familiar with how fast it drains during normal activity so you know how much you're holding in reserve for emergencies.
Look for your first blueprint — blueprints can appear near the surface, so keep an eye out early rather than assuming they're only found deep down.

## Progression

Wood → Iron → Steel → Greenstone Gold → Diamond → Ruby → Titanium
Each tier unlocks stronger tools and armor, letting you mine faster and survive deeper layers.
Rock layers get tougher to mine the deeper you go, so keep your tools upgraded to match — trying to push past your tool tier will slow you down significantly.
Blueprints found at each depth tend to correspond to the materials around them, so mining efficiently through a tier helps you unlock shop items tied to that stage.
Explore caves carefully — bring torches, food, and weapons, since cave systems often branch off unpredictably and can lead you further from your base than expected.
Keep an eye out for rare items: Amulet of Flight, Ten League Boots, Shield Badge, and Water Talisman — these aren't guaranteed finds, so treat them as bonus goals rather than requirements for progressing.

## Mining Tips

Use staircases to move safely — staircases let you retreat quickly if needed and give you steady control over your descent.
Ropes are the fastest way down — if you need to descend quickly, ropes beat staircases, though they trade off some of the safety staircases offer.
Mark paths with torches or signs — this prevents you from getting lost in branching tunnels, especially once you're several tiers deep.
Carry spare tools and blocks for emergency building — a broken pickaxe with no backup can strand you deep underground.
Watch for mobs spawning at night — this applies below ground too, not just on the surface, since unlit tunnels are just as vulnerable.
Manage your stamina — don't sprint everywhere; keep enough in reserve to escape if you run into something you can't fight.
Don't dig straight down — you risk strand you deep underground.

## Base Building

Build near resources but in a safe area — ideally close enough to your mining shafts to cut down on travel time, but not so close that cave openings are right next to your walls.
Add storage, crafting stations, and defensive walls — organize storage early so it doesn't become chaotic as you collect more materials and blueprints.
Keep beds and lighting to avoid mobs — a bed also lets you reset your spawn point, which matters more the deeper your base is.
Stock up at the Block Shop and Item Shop for anything you're missing — as you unlock more with blueprints, revisit the shops often since new gear becomes available.
Consider building rest points at major depth milestones — a small lit shelter every so often gives you a safe fallback if you need to recover stamina or escape a fight.

## Blueprints & Unlocks
Blueprints are scattered throughout the depths and are required to unlock items in the Block Shop and Item Shop.
Since almost everything starts locked, prioritizing blueprint collection early opens up better tools, blocks, and gear sooner.
Some blueprints may be tied to specific depths or material tiers, so don't rush past an area too quickly — take time to explore before moving deeper.
