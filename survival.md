## Overview

Survival mode focuses on staying alive against enemies while managing resources and building a secure base.

---

## Early Game Survival

- Find **shelter** before night.  
- Craft **basic tools** and **weapons**.  
- Keep **food** and **light** nearby.

---

## Crafting Essentials

- Tools: pickaxe, axe, shovel, sword  
- Utility: torches, chests, doors  
- Defense: walls, traps, armor  

---

## Combat Tips

- Avoid fighting multiple enemies at once.  
- Use **terrain** to your advantage.  
- Keep **healing items** ready.

---

## Base Defense

- Build **walls** and **gates**.  
- Use **lighting** to reduce enemy spawns.  
- Add **traps** and **elevated positions**.

---

## Long-Term Goals

- Expand your base  
- Automate resource gathering  
- Explore dangerous areas with better gear
