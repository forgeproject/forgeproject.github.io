## Overview

Survival mode is built for long-term play — you're not racing to the bottom, you're settling in. Maps can span up to a massive 8192x8192 blocks, giving you far more room to explore, build, and expand than you'd get elsewhere. Depth is much shallower here too, capped at 510 blocks, so mining is more about steady resource gathering than a deep descent. Everything in the shops is unlocked from the start, so there's no blueprint hunting required — just gather what you need and buy it. The real goal is staying alive: manage your resources, build a base that can defend itself, and grow it over time.

---

## Early Game Survival

- **Find shelter before night** — even a basic enclosed space is enough to keep early mobs out while you get your bearings. Don't wander too far from it until you know the surrounding area.
- **Craft basic tools and weapons** — a pickaxe and sword should be your first priority so you can gather resources and defend yourself at the same time.
- **Keep food and light nearby** — hunger and darkness are the two things that'll get you killed fastest early on, so stock both before you start exploring.
- **Scout your surroundings** — with maps this large, take a little time early to figure out what resources are nearby (forests, stone, water) before committing to a base location.
- **Visit the shops early** — since everything's unlocked from the start, it's worth checking what's available right away rather than waiting until you're deeper into the game.

---

## Crafting Essentials

- **Tools:** pickaxe, axe, shovel, sword — these cover gathering and basic defense, and you'll want upgraded versions of each as you find better materials.
- **Utility:** torches, chests, doors — torches keep areas lit and mob-free, chests keep your growing resource pile organized, and doors control who (or what) gets in.
- **Defense:** walls, traps, armor — armor should be a priority alongside weapons, since Survival is about staying alive over the long haul, not just surviving one encounter.
- **Plan for upgrades** — since the shops have everything unlocked, don't hesitate to buy better tools or materials as soon as you can afford them rather than waiting to "need" them.

---

## Combat Tips

- **Avoid fighting multiple enemies at once** — retreat to a chokepoint or doorway if you're getting swarmed rather than standing your ground in the open.
- **Use terrain to your advantage** — high ground, narrow paths, and natural barriers can all help you fight one enemy at a time instead of getting surrounded.
- **Keep healing items ready** — always carry a backup supply, especially if you're exploring far from your base where retreating isn't an option.
- **Don't overextend** — with a map this large, it's easy to wander far from safety chasing a fight or a resource. Know how far you are from shelter before you commit.

---

## Base Defense

- **Build walls and gates** — a fully enclosed base is the best defense against mobs, and gates let you control access without leaving gaps.
- **Use lighting to reduce enemy spawns** — light up not just your base interior but the area immediately around it, since unlit ground nearby can still spawn mobs.
- **Add traps and elevated positions** — traps thin out mobs before they reach your walls, and elevated spots give you a safe vantage point to fight from.
- **Plan for expansion** — since maps can stretch up to 8192x8192, build with room to grow. A base that's boxed in early gets harder to expand later.
- **Section off your base** — as it grows, separate areas for storage, crafting, and living space keep things organized and easier to defend piece by piece.

## Skills Mode

When creating your world, you can enable Skills Mode — either locally for a single world or globally across all your worlds. With it turned on, you won't have full access to mining or using everything right away; instead, you'll need to level up individual skills before you can make full use of them.

- **How it works** — each skill starts low and levels up as you use it, gradually unlocking what you can mine, build, or use tied to that skill. Until a skill is leveled up enough, certain blocks, tools, or actions tied to it stay restricted.
- **Local vs. Global** — a local setting only affects the world it's enabled on, while a global setting applies your skill levels across every world you play, so progress carries over.
- **Skills you can level:**
  - Combat
  - Health
  - Strength
  - Attack
  - Defence
  - Ranged
  - Mining
  - Digging
  - Chopping
  - Building
  - Crafting
  - Smelting
  - Smithing
  - Farming
  - Cooking
  - Looting
- **Plan around it** — since this mode gates what you can do until you've leveled up, factor early playtime into building up the skills you'll rely on most, whether that's Mining and Digging for resource gathering or Combat and Defence for surviving encounters.

---

## Long-Term Goals

- **Expand your base** — with the map size you're working with, there's plenty of room to grow outward or even establish secondary outposts.
- **Automate resource gathering** — set up efficient farming, mining, or storage systems so you're spending less time on upkeep and more time building or exploring.
- **Explore dangerous areas with better gear** — once your base is stable and your gear is upgraded, push into riskier areas for rarer resources.
- **Build for the long haul** — Survival mode rewards steady growth over time, so treat your base as an ongoing project rather than something to "finish."
